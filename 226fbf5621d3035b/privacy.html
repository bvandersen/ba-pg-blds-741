Rail Yard – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Rail Yard is a train-set game: you draw track and run trains on it. It is made to be usable by young children.

What the app collects

Nothing. Rail Yard does not collect, store, send or share any personal information or any other data about you or your device. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. It doesn't ask for any permissions, and it doesn't use the internet at all.

What stays on your device

The layouts you save and the game's settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. They never leave it and are not included in device backups. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Children

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children. There are no links out of the app, no chat and no ads.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does, and the date above will change.

Questions: use the developer email address shown on Rail Yard's Google Play page.
